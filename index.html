Class 3rd to 5th Arithmetic

Fast Addition Without Finger Counting

We teach friendly number rounding (मित्र संख्या विधि) so kids calculate in their heads in 2 seconds.

The Question

48 + 37 = ?

Step 1: Round 48 to 50 by borrowing 2 from 37 (48 + 2 = 50).

Step 2: Remaining from 37 is 35 (37 - 2 = 35).

Final Answer: 50 + 35 = 85! Solved in 2 seconds!
